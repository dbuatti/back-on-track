A note on waking
  • Keep wake time roughly the same each day — a steady cue helps the body clock, even after a rough night.
  • Step into daylight within the first hour. Morning light anchors the rhythm better than anything else.
  • If you wake at 3am, don't check the clock. Low light, no problem-solving — leave it until morning.
